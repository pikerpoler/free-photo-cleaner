# Privacy Policy for AI Photo Cleaner

**Last updated: November 10, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Photo Cleaner (the “App”) handles information when you use it.

## Information the App Accesses

The App requests access to your photo library so that you can review, organize, and delete photos and videos.

The App may process:

- Photos and videos that you permit it to access.
- Media information such as file names, file sizes, dimensions, creation dates, and video durations.
- Whether a file appears to be a screenshot or a WhatsApp image.
- Your decisions to keep or delete media.
- Device storage totals, media counts, and your App settings.
- Numerical photo features for optional on-device AI sorting, including brightness and color statistics, object-category scores, the count and approximate placement of detected faces, and the amount of the image that looks like text. These features do not include faceprints, people's identities, or words from the image.

## On-Device Processing

Photo analysis, AI model training, and sorting take place locally on your device. The App does not transmit your photos, videos, media metadata, swipe history, or AI features to us or to a third-party AI service.

The App uses Apple's on-device Vision framework and an AI model bundled with the App. It does not perform facial recognition or attempt to identify anyone. Text-like regions are measured, but the words in your images are not read or stored.

## Information We Collect

We do not operate a backend service and do not automatically receive information from the App.

The App does not include:

- Advertising.
- Analytics or telemetry.
- Tracking technologies.
- User accounts.
- Transmission of photos to third-party AI services.
- Sale or sharing of personal information.

## Information Stored on Your Device

To provide its features, the App stores information locally on your device, including:

- Media identifiers and metadata.
- Items marked as kept or pending deletion.
- Swipe history.
- App preferences and AI model configuration.
- Numerical AI training features and keep-or-delete labels.
- Trained AI models and temporary processing caches.

AI training examples may remain stored after the related photo has been deleted. You can delete the AI training dataset and reset trained models from within the App.

Removing the App generally removes its locally stored data. Copies may remain in device backups controlled by your operating system or backup provider.

## Deleting Photos and Videos

When you confirm deletion, the App asks the operating system to delete the selected media. Deleted items may remain in your system's “Recently Deleted,” trash, or equivalent folder according to your device settings and photo-library provider.

## Permissions

Photo-library access is used only to display, analyze, organize, and delete media at your request. You can change this permission in your device settings. Restricting access may prevent some or all of the App's features from working.

## Bug Reports and Contact

When you choose to report a bug, the App opens your email application with a prefilled subject and message template. The App does not automatically attach photos, photo-library information, diagnostics, or device logs. We receive only the information you choose to include and send.

If you contact us, we use the information you provide to respond to your message and address your request.

## External Services

The App may open the Buy Me a Coffee website when you choose to donate. Your browser, email provider, Buy Me a Coffee, and any other external service you choose to use are governed by their own privacy policies and practices.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children. It may process photos containing children as part of a user's photo library, but those photos and any derived features are processed only on the device and are not transmitted to us.

## Security

Keeping photo processing on your device limits the exposure of your information. However, no device or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and choosing appropriate backup settings.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's features or information-handling practices change. The updated policy will be identified by a revised “Last updated” date.
